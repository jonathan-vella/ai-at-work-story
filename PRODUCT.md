# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary readers are infrastructure architects and engineers considering how AI could extend their work. Other professional readers interested in a personal account of changing work practices are also in scope.

## Product Purpose

The site presents Jonathan Vella's first-person account of how AI has helped him extend his work as a Microsoft Azure infrastructure architect. Success means an infrastructure architect finishes the page willing to try one thing this week and believing their experience still matters, while understanding the human judgment, verification, and specialist input that remain necessary.

## Positioning

The story uses specific personal examples and states their evidence limits. It is not generic AI guidance, a controlled productivity study, or a claim that AI makes one person an expert in every specialty.

## Operating Context

The site is a static Astro website configured for GitHub Pages. `story.mdx` is the canonical narrative, `evidence.md` records claim status and limitations, and the story's visuals are Astro components that take their words from `story.mdx`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site presents the approved personal narrative and its supporting visuals.
- Keep approved public wording and its claim limitations intact. New public claims or material require Jonathan's approval.
- Do not include material from `.private-research/` or imply that self-reported results are independently verified or generalizable.
- The site is a personal account, not official Microsoft guidance or endorsement.

## Brand Commitments

Use the first-person personal voice of the story. Preserve its distinction between Jonathan's own experience and general advice.

## Evidence on Hand

- `story.mdx`: the approved narrative.
- `evidence.md`: approved claims, public references, limitations, and evidence gaps.
- `public/images/`: the photos, the Factory lab and APEX demo diagrams, and the share card.
- `captions.md`: their captions and accessible descriptions.

Do not invent testimonials, benchmarks, customer outcomes, or proof beyond the approved story and evidence.

## Product Principles

- Ground claims in the approved personal account and state material limitations.
- Show how AI can extend existing expertise without presenting it as a replacement for human judgment.
- Make verification, specialist collaboration, and production responsibility clear.
- Keep the story and its supporting visuals understandable and accessible.
