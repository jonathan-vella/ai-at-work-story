## Questions to develop next

1. What is the first thing you built with AI that you would not realistically have attempted before, and what had held you back?
2. Which single example best represents the change from days or weeks to one day? What was included, and what still happened outside that day?
3. What did your first useful agent-assisted workflow look like, and what did you have to correct?
4. How did APEX start? Which repeated problem were you trying to solve for yourself?
5. What made you change the earlier APEX approach, and what did that teach you about using AI?
6. How did the Modernization Factory work begin, and what was your contribution?
7. What can a colleague or partner now do with something you built that previously depended on your direct involvement?
8. What evidence supports that benefit, and which details or examples may be made public?
9. How has your work moved beyond your previous infrastructure focus, if it has?
10. Which decisions do you refuse to delegate to AI? Describe a time that judgement mattered.
11. What has AI made harder, slower or more expensive?
12. What work did the time saved actually allow you to do?
13. What would you recommend another architect try first, and how should they check whether it helped?
14. What final public format best fits the story once the narrative is established?
