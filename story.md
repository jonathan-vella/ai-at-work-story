This is an unfinished structure. The opening facts below were approved for this repository. Prompts are gaps to resolve, not claims.

## Before May 2025

I work as a Microsoft Azure infrastructure architect.

A piece of architecture work could involve capturing and documenting business outcomes, non-functional requirements, compliance and regulatory requirements, budget and timelines. Then came the initial architecture, cost estimates, a Well-Architected review, alignment with the customer's Azure Policy constraints, infrastructure as code, deployment, adjustments and documentation.

Before AI, that could take me days or weeks.

To develop: one representative example, the main bottleneck, and what I could not realistically take on alongside that work.

## The first change

In May 2025, I discovered that agents in GitHub could help me create architecture diagrams, Azure cost estimates and Well-Architected reviews much faster.

Azure MCP became part of my experience in June-July 2025. Skills followed in late 2025.

These dates describe my experience, not claims about when those technologies launched.

To develop: the first useful result, what surprised me, and what I had to fix.

## What became faster

Today, I can complete that architecture-to-deployment-and-documentation workflow in one day.

This is my account of my own work, not a controlled benchmark or a promise that every project takes one day. The comparison still needs a concrete example and a clear description of what was included.
