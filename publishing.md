# Publication rules

This repository is public. Do not use it to hold material awaiting a publication decision.

## Jonathan decides what may be included

Ask Jonathan about specific material rather than deciding its publication status on his behalf.

Access to a source, an existing public URL, a filename containing "Public", a sensitivity label or removal of names does not substitute for his approval to include it in this story.

If a source carries restrictions or requires another party's permission, explain that before proceeding. Jonathan's review does not itself override source restrictions or another person's rights.

That approval does not cover later interview answers, research findings or expanded claims.

## Material not included in the initial approval

No email content, internal role-playbook content, internal links, third-party quotes or customer/partner details are approved for this repository.

Do not put those materials into files, commit messages, issues, pull requests, screenshots or attachments. Do not add them first and ask for approval afterward.

Keep research involving Microsoft 365 and the supplied role playbook in the private conversation unless Jonathan approves a specific proposed addition and applicable restrictions permit it.

## Approval process for additions

1. Show the exact proposed text, links, images and claims in the private conversation.
2. Explain the source, any known restrictions, evidence limitations and intended public destination.
3. Ask Jonathan to approve that specific addition.
4. Add only what he approved and record the scope and date.

An interview answer is not publication approval. Neither is approval of a general theme.

## Writing and evidence

Apply the Unslop skill throughout. Preserve Jonathan's voice and meaning. Remove generic AI prose without inventing detail.

Label personal recollections as such. Do not fabricate metrics, imply that a preview is production-ready, or claim adoption or customer benefit merely because code or documentation exists.

Distinguish AI assistance from Jonathan's judgement and collaborators' contributions.

Obtain approval for the complete final version and its destination before publication elsewhere.
